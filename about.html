About Halden

A studio built around listening.

We opened Halden because skin care had become loud and rushed. Our answer is simple: more time, fewer products, and honest advice.

12+Years of combined practice

Our mission

Care that respects your time and your skin.

Two promises guide every decision we make in the studio.

Honest advice

If a treatment won't help, we'll tell you — and suggest what will, even if that's a simpler routine at home.

Long-term thinking

We plan in seasons, not single visits, so progress is steady and your skin barrier stays healthy.

Our approach
STEP 01

Listen

A relaxed first conversation about your skin history, habits and what you'd like to change.

STEP 02

Assess

A close skin analysis under good light, so the plan is based on what we see — not guesswork.

STEP 03

Treat

Focused treatments at the right intensity, with clear explanations at every step.

STEP 04

Follow up

Check-ins and small adjustments, so results keep building between visits.
