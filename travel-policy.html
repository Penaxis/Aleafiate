Travel policy.

What to check before you travel with a prescribed medication, within Australia or overseas.

Disclaimer — Travel with Medicinal Cannabis

Patients prescribed Medicinal Cannabis are advised that laws relating to cannabis differ between countries and jurisdictions, including within Australia and overseas.

It is the patient’s responsibility to check the legal status of Medicinal Cannabis in any destination they plan to travel to before flying or carrying their medication.

Please note:

  • This information is provided as a general guide only and is not medical advice.
  • This disclaimer is issued by the clinic for informational purposes and does not come from a doctor.
  • Regulations around Medicinal Cannabis vary widely, and what is legally prescribed in Australia may be restricted or prohibited elsewhere.

Before travelling, patients should

  • Contact the embassy or consulate of their destination country for up-to-date legal guidance.
  • Check airline and airport policies regarding the carriage of cannabis-based medications.
  • Ensure they carry their medication in original packaging, clearly labelled with their name and prescription details.
  • Carry a copy of their prescription and, if required, a letter from the prescribing clinic (available upon request).

Important: Always seek clarification from relevant authorities.
