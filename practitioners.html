How prescribing works

Safe, simple and never rushed.

Prescription treatments are medicines, so they follow a clear medical process. Here’s what to expect.

Prescribing FAQs
01Step 01

Consultation

A relaxed, in-person conversation about your skin, health history, medication and goals.

02Step 02

Medical assessment

Your prescriber examines your skin and checks that a prescription treatment is safe and suitable for you.

03Step 03

Your plan

If it’s right for you, you get a written plan with the treatment, the cost, any risks and aftercare. No pressure to decide on the day.

04Step 04

Review & follow-up

We check in after treatment and review your progress, adjusting the plan as your skin changes.
