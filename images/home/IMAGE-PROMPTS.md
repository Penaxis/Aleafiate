# Homepage images — sizes and AI prompts

Generate each image, save it with the **exact file name** below into `images/home/`,
and it appears on the homepage automatically. Until a file exists, its slot shows a
labelled placeholder.

**File format:** JPG (or WebP), quality ~80, ideally under 400 KB each.
**Sizes** are the minimum to stay sharp on large screens. Bigger is fine if the shape (ratio) matches.

---

## Before you start — rules for every image

**Add this style line to the end of every prompt** so the set looks consistent:

> Editorial lifestyle photography, soft natural daylight, calm and reassuring mood, colour palette of deep navy, teal and soft mint with warm neutral tones, modern Australian setting, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

**Add this to the "negative prompt" box** (or to the end of the prompt as "Avoid: …"):

> cannabis leaves or plants, marijuana, hemp, pills, capsules, medicine bottles, oils, droppers, syringes, smoking, vaping, before-and-after, distressed or crying people, hospital beds, text, logos

**Why the restrictions:** Australian advertising rules (TGA) don't allow a clinic's
website to show or hint at prescription medicines, and that includes plant imagery that
suggests them. AHPRA also requires that images don't mislead. So:

- **Never use AI images of your real practitioners.** Dr Frank and Jeremy need real photos (`images/team/`).
- **The clinic photos (section 5) should ideally be real photos of your clinic** at Logan Central Plaza. AI versions are fine as temporary stand-ins.
- People in AI images should look like everyday Australians of different ages and backgrounds, and calm or comfortable, never unwell or in pain.
