Request a free call or phone 07 3473 3396.

Prescriptions & scripts

A prescribing practitioner is a qualified, registered clinician — such as a doctor or nurse practitioner — who is legally allowed to prescribe medicines. Some treatments can only be supplied after a prescriber has assessed you.

After your consultation, prescriptions are sent directly to your chosen pharmacy by email within 12–24 hours, along with any required TGA approvals.

Yes. If a treatment isn’t safe or suitable for you, your practitioner won’t go ahead — and they’ll explain why and talk you through other options.

Please tell us about any medicines you take. We use eScript software to check for conflicts with prescriptions from other practitioners. If a conflict is found, the script may be rejected and the consultation fee forfeited.

Some treatments can affect your ability to drive or operate machinery, and some can interact with other medicines. Your practitioner will explain anything that applies to you — please follow their advice and report any side effects promptly. See our Terms and Conditions.
