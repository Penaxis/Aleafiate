Your first visit

Your first visit is a 45-minute consultation. We talk through your skin history, routine, health and goals, then look closely at your skin under good light.

You leave with a written plan. We only treat on the day if it is clearly the right thing to do.

No need. We cleanse your skin before any assessment or treatment. If you can, skip heavy makeup and come without fake tan on the area being treated.

A list of the products you use (photos of the labels are fine), any medication you take, and details of past treatments or skin conditions. It helps us plan safely.

About ten minutes before your appointment, so you have time to settle in and fill in a short health form.

02

Treatments

That is exactly what the consultation is for. We match treatments to how your skin behaves, your lifestyle and your budget, and we will tell you honestly if a simpler routine at home would work just as well.

Most of our facials and LED sessions are relaxing. Peels can tingle for a few minutes, and microneedling feels like light scratching. We use numbing cream where it helps, and you can ask us to pause at any time.

It depends on the treatment:

  • Facials and LED: none.
  • Light peels: mild redness for a few hours.
  • Microneedling: redness for 24–48 hours.

We will always explain this before you book.

Some treatments work well on their own; others, like microneedling, are planned as a course of three to six sessions a few weeks apart. Your plan will set this out clearly, including the cost.

03

Prescription treatments

A prescribing practitioner is a qualified, registered clinician — a doctor, nurse or pharmacist — who is legally allowed to prescribe medicines. Some skin treatments can only be supplied after a prescriber has assessed you.

Meet our prescribing practitioners.

Prescription treatments are medicines. A prescriber must assess you in person, check your medical history and make sure the treatment is safe and suitable before anything is prescribed.

Yes. If a treatment is not safe or not right for you, we will not go ahead — and we will explain why and suggest alternatives. Your wellbeing always comes before a booking.

Many prescription treatments are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please tell us if either applies to you, and we will suggest gentle, suitable options instead.

Only with your consent. We may ask to contact your GP if we need more information about your health, or recommend that you share your treatment details with them.

04

Aftercare & results

Some changes, like a brighter, calmer complexion, show within days. Deeper changes to texture or tone usually take four to twelve weeks, as skin renews itself.

You will get written aftercare for every treatment. In most cases that means gentle products, daily SPF and avoiding heat, exercise or active ingredients for a short time.

Contact us straight away by phone or email and we will advise you, or bring you back in to be seen. Every treatment includes a follow-up check.

Yes. Follow-ups are part of every treatment plan, so we can check your progress and adjust the plan if needed.
